# What Budget Buddy Does NOT Do

## Overview
Budget Buddy is designed strictly as a manual personal budgeting, goal tracking, and loan management assistant. To ensure total transparency and protect user privacy, the following capabilities are intentionally **NOT** supported by the app:

## 1. No Automatic Bank Account Linking
Budget Buddy does not connect to your bank accounts, netbanking portals, or Account Aggregator networks. All transactions must be manually logged or imported by the user.

## 2. No SMS Reading or Notification Scraping
The app does not request Android or iOS SMS permissions, does not read incoming text messages, and does not parse transaction OTPs or bank debit alerts.

## 3. No Payments or Fund Transfers
Budget Buddy is not a payment app or digital wallet. You cannot transfer money, make UPI payments, pay utility bills, or execute transactions through the app.

## 4. No Credit Score or CIBIL Inquiries
The app does not pull credit scores, does not perform credit inquiries with bureaus (such as CIBIL, Experian, or Equifax), and does not report loan repayment statuses to credit agencies.

## 5. No Stock, Crypto, or Investment Trading
While Budget Buddy allows you to record your own investment portfolios and calculate returns, it does not connect to brokerages (such as Zerodha or Groww) and cannot execute buy or sell orders for stocks, mutual funds, or cryptocurrencies.

## 6. No Automated Data Modifications by AI
The AI Coach has read-only access to user data. It cannot create, edit, or delete transactions, budgets, or loan records on your behalf.
