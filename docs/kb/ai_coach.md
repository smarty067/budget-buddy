# Budget Buddy AI Coach

## Overview
Budget Buddy features an in-app AI Coach designed to help you understand your spending patterns, plan budgets, track savings goals, and manage loan repayments. It provides short, practical financial insights formatted in Indian Rupees (₹).

## What the AI Coach Can See
When you ask a question about your finances, the coach can look up relevant data stored inside Budget Buddy:
- **Transactions**: Expenses and income recorded in the app for the current and previous month.
- **Budgets**: Monthly category spending limits and how much you have spent against each budget.
- **Savings Goals**: Target amounts, deadlines, and current saved progress.
- **Saved Loans**: Active loans you have saved, including principal, interest rate, tenure, and monthly EMI.
- **Profile Preferences**: Stated monthly income, currency, and language preference.

## What the AI Coach CANNOT See
To protect your privacy, the AI Coach does not have access to external systems:
- It **cannot** see your live bank balances.
- It **cannot** read your SMS messages or OTPs.
- It **cannot** view or connect to external bank accounts, credit card accounts, or UPI apps.
- It **cannot** modify, delete, or create transactions on your behalf.

## How Information is Looked Up
The coach uses on-demand retrieval. It only looks up your data when your specific question requires it:
- If you say "hello" or ask a general personal finance concept (such as what a mutual fund is), **no personal financial data is retrieved**.
- If you ask about your spending or loan payments, the coach looks up only that specific section of your data to calculate your answer.
- All numbers quoted by the coach are based directly on the records you entered in Budget Buddy. Outstanding loan amounts are mathematical estimates based on your tenure and EMI schedule.

## Supported Languages
You can converse with the AI Coach in English, Hindi, Hinglish, or Kannada.
