# Loan and EMI Management

## Overview
Budget Buddy includes an EMI Calculator and loan tracker to help you plan debt obligations and see how EMIs fit into your monthly budget.

## Calculating an EMI
1. Open the **Loan Calculator** from the app dashboard.
2. Enter or adjust:
   - **Loan Amount**: Total principal amount borrowed.
   - **Interest Rate**: Annual percentage interest rate (p.a.).
   - **Tenure**: Duration of the loan in years or months.
3. The calculator instantly displays your monthly payable EMI, total interest payable, and total payment over the loan tenure.
4. You can also view the complete month-by-month repayment schedule by tapping **View Amortization Schedule**.

## Saving a Loan to Your Portfolio
Once you have calculated an EMI:
1. Tap **SAVE AS LOAN** at the bottom of the calculator.
2. Enter a friendly name for the loan (for example, "HDFC Home Loan" or "Car Loan").
3. Choose the start date when payments began (defaults to today).
4. Tap **Confirm & Save Loan**.

The loan is now saved to your active portfolio so that both your dashboard and the AI Coach can take your monthly EMI commitments into account.

## Viewing and Managing Saved Loans
Access your saved loans anytime by tapping the **My Loans** icon in the Loan Calculator:
- **Active Loans**: Displays all ongoing loans with monthly EMI, tenure remaining, and estimated outstanding balance.
- **Mark as Closed**: When you finish repaying a loan or prepay it in full, tap the options menu on the loan card and select **Mark as Closed**. Closed loans move to the Closed tab and their EMI is no longer counted in your active commitments.
- **Delete a Loan**: If you added a loan by mistake, select **Delete Loan** from the card options to remove it permanently.

## How Outstanding Balances are Estimated
The estimated outstanding balance shown in Budget Buddy is calculated based on standard reducing-balance loan amortization assuming all scheduled EMIs have been paid on time. If you made extra prepayments or missed payments with your bank, the actual balance with your lender may differ.
